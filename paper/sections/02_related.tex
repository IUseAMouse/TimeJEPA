\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

Zero-shot forecasting spans several architectural paradigms, none
dominant: decoder-only (TimesFM \citep{das2024timesfm}, Toto
\citep{cohen2024toto}), masked encoders (Moirai \citep{woo2024moirai},
whose LOTSA corpus we pretrain on), tokenized seq-to-seq (Chronos
\citep{ansari2024chronos}), xLSTM (TiRex \citep{auer2025tirex}), state
space models (FlowState \citep{flowstate2025}), and MLP mixers (TTM
\citep{ekambaram2024ttm}), all competitive on GIFT-Eval
\citep{aksu2024gifteval}. Parameter count is demonstrably not the lever
there: Toto spans 4M to 1B parameters for 4.6 CRPS points, and sub-10M
models beat far larger ones. That is TimeJEPA's class, and why testing
which pretraining objective serves forecasting at fixed small capacity has
a place.

JEPA was proposed as a path to predictive world models
\citep{lecun2022path} and instantiated for images and video
\citep{assran2023ijepa,bardes2024vjepa,assran2025vjepa2}, always with a
disposable predictor; anti-collapse machinery comes from BYOL's EMA
target \citep{grill2020byol,chen2021mocov3}, VICReg
\citep{bardes2022vicreg}, and LeJEPA's SIGReg
\citep{balestriero2025lejepa}, our default. Our energy reading of the
latent follows \citet{lecun2006ebm}. To our knowledge TimeJEPA is the
first systematic attempt to keep the JEPA predictor as the forecasting
organ for time series and measure whether that helps.

Four components carry load-bearing precedents: patching and channel
independence from PatchTST \citep{nie2023patchtst}, instance
normalization from RevIN \citep{kim2022revin} with the robust arcsinh
precedent in Toto \citep{cohen2024toto}, synthetic pretraining data from
KernelSynth \citep{ansari2024chronos} via random Fourier features
\citep{rahimi2007rff}, and sign-flip TTA from TimesFM and YingLong
\citep{das2024timesfm,yinglong2025}. Remaining components and the
methods entering our negative results are cited where used.
