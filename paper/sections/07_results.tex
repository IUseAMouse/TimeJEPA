\paragraph{Checkpoint selection by evaluation, not validation.}
Champions are selected by evaluating checkpoints every 5\% of the
fine-tuning epoch, because validation loss designates the wrong checkpoint,
three times out of three, including once with a perfectly clean, stationary,
monotone validation curve (best-val checkpoint \gpair{0.896}{0.631} vs the
45\% checkpoint \gpair{0.895}{0.624}). Fine-tuning validation and zero-shot
transfer measure different things. The selection is honest: fixed
checkpoint grid, all 97 configurations, both TTA variants, never a
per-configuration choice.

\subsection{Where the gap lives}
\label{sec:gap}

Two floors. The \emph{tail}: sixteen configurations above ratio 1.25 cost
$\sim$12 aggregate points before the mix recipe; the recipe compressed them
to six ($\sim$7 points), exactly as pre-registered, recovering the
predicted configurations (solar 10T from $\times$2.1--2.4 to 0.94--0.99;
ETT/electricity 15T from $\times$1.3--1.45 to 0.78--0.98). The residual
tail (bizitobs 10S, electricity/15T/long, us\_births) is what the v3
synthetic families target. The \emph{body}: on the other 81 configurations
we sit near 0.54 where the sub-10M leaders sit near 0.47, diffuse, no
single culprit. Terms are flat (short 0.611, medium 0.619, long 0.616): the
rollout is not the bottleneck. Durable strengths: solar weekly beats every
model on the board including the 2.5B ones (ratios 0.43--0.75); m\_dense
hourly beats Toto-4m; bitbrains beats TTM and FlowState.

\subsection{v3 against its registered bands}

Registered before the first checkpoint: plain CRPS success $\le 0.590$,
flip $\le 0.566$, failure diagnostic at plain $> 0.605$. At the 50\%
checkpoint: MASE 0.8633 flip, best of the project; CRPS 0.5983 flip, 0.24
points off the champion and short of the registered band. P-v3.2
(short-series block) was formally weakened at assembly when geometry proved
the point (Section~\ref{sec:corpus}). \pending{v3-final: 75--100\%
checkpoints, verdicts on P-v3.1 (bizitobs $<1.0$), P-v3.3
(electricity/15T/long $<1.0$), P-v3.4, and the per-configuration autopsy.}

\subsection{Triple convergence = a capacity ceiling}
\label{sec:ceiling}

Three fine-tunes from three pretrains on three corpora (rationed mix,
ESJEPA, v3) land at flip CRPS 0.5959, 0.5981, 0.5983: $0.597 \pm 0.002$.
Each varied a lever that had previously moved the aggregate; none crossed
the floor. The last orthogonal lever, a native h512 fine-tune from the
same pretrain (grown query table), confirmed rather than threatened the
reading: it plateaus at \gpair{0.892}{0.612} with flip, its deficit fully
accounted for by a mechanical side effect (the 1536-step training window
drops the decimated 10-minute block and the padded short-series block from
the fine-tuning corpus, costing $+11.1\%$ on the 21 affected
configurations) plus a horizon effect too small to matter
(Section~\ref{sec:hypothesis}). It also produced the best-calibrated
checkpoint of the project as a side effect, empirical 80\% coverage of
exactly 0.800, plausibly because fine-tuning under horizon randomization
up to 512 steps teaches the head to calibrate across horizons; we flag
that ingredient for future recipes. Reading: at 1.14M parameters the model
is capacity-bound at this corpus quality, and the next experiment is
scale, one variable, with registered success and failure bands.
