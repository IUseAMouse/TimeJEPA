\section{The latent as judge}
\label{sec:judge}

The objective is not where forecasting accuracy comes from. Here is where
the latent earns its keep. A pretrained JEPA is implicitly an energy
function \citep{lecun2006ebm}
\begin{equation}
E(x, y) = 1 - \cos\!\big(\hat{z}(x),\ \mathrm{enc}([x \,\|\, y])\big),
\end{equation}
lowered on true pairs by the invariance term. Everything below reads this
energy off \emph{frozen} checkpoints: no training on downstream data,
anywhere.

\subsection{The energy probe}
\label{sec:probe}

Six GIFT configurations $\times$ 100 instances $\times$ 34 candidates (32
block-bootstraps, seasonal naive, the truth); the metric is the normalized
rank of the true future (chance 0.50). Table~\ref{tab:probe} and
Figure~\ref{fig:probe}: pretrained checkpoints are competent judges, and
fine-tuning \emph{destroys} the judge.

A second regularity, replicated on three lineages and promoted to a working
law: \emph{the judge peaks early}. Best-judge checkpoints sat at mid-run,
at 15\% and at 5\% of their schedules, then eroded while validation kept
improving. Forecaster and judge have disjoint temporal optima on the same
trajectory; judge selection uses an early cheap probe, never validation and
never GIFT.

\subsection{Propose, judge, reweight}
\label{sec:pjr}

The judge becomes useful through a zero-training pipeline: propose
candidate futures (block-bootstraps at two block scales, anchors,
optionally an external model's paths), score with $E$, softmax the
\emph{standardized} energies (scale-free, so v0 has no tunable
temperature), read weighted quantiles. Table~\ref{tab:hybrid} gives the
numbers on six Nixtla datasets.

Three mechanisms behind the table. The bootstrap's failure is its envelope
(it recombines the past; exchange trends out of it), which the decoder
repairs as proposer. Candidate refinement by a few gradient steps on $E$
(planning by backprop, small step count as a Goodhart guard) adds 1--2\%
where the envelope binds; on the centered pool below, where no envelope
binds, we measured it null on five datasets and slightly harmful on the
trending sixth, so refinement and centering are substitutes, not
complements, and the official pipeline uses centering alone. And the deliverable
is \emph{uplift over the proposer}: a verifier for forecasts, not a
competing forecaster. Generation is hard, verification is easy; a frozen
judge that improves a state-of-the-art proposer with zero dedicated
training is, to our knowledge, a novel use of a JEPA latent in time
series.

\paragraph{Killing the dilution was a matter of center, not of weighting.}
The dilution signature survived a scalar temperature, a calibrated
temperature, and three proposer changes; it died the day the candidates
changed \emph{center}. A history bootstrap is centered on the past, so
every gram of weight it receives pulls the weighted median away from the
proposer's trend. The fix in the bottom block of Table~\ref{tab:hybrid}
bootstraps seasonal innovations $y_t - y_{t-m}$ and re-attaches the blocks
to the proposer's path: spread without a competing center. Registered
predictions held on both counts (the two dilution datasets recover; the
four wins keep or grow their margins), and one safety property survives by
design: seasonal naive stays in the pool as an alternative center, and in
a controlled test with a deliberately biased proposer the judge moves its
mass there, three standard deviations of preference. A verifier that can
fire its proposer is worth having.

Two negative measurements bound the claim. Transplanting the pipeline
naively onto the GIFT windows failed, mechanistically: a TTM-paths-only
pool collapses because TTM contracts input jitter (near-identical paths,
fan degenerating to a point, 80\% coverage 0.206), and near-identical
energies turn the standardized softmax into a noise amplifier that dumps
mass on anchor candidates, with localized disasters exactly on the daily
and weekly configurations where a linear drift anchor diverges. Both
mechanisms are now guarded (the drift anchor left the pool, degenerate
energies fall back to uniform weights), and the centered pool is the
registered candidate for a GIFT rerun; until measured there, the claim
stays protocol-scoped to single-shot horizons. The TTM-alone GIFT column
doubles as harness validation (MASE ratio 0.765 on capped windows against
the official 0.724), and one side finding: TTM emits NaN on 100\% of two
sparse count configurations (hierarchical\_sales and restaurant, daily)
where our own model runs unaided.

\subsection{ESJEPA: predict the statistics of what cannot be predicted}
\label{sec:esjepa}

The predictor converges to a conditional mean and carries no dispersion
(Section~\ref{sec:quantile-head}). The fix is not predicting the residual
(irrecoverable) but its \emph{statistics}: a 4-dimensional deterministic
target per patch (log-RMS and log-MAD of the residual around a causal EWMA,
bounded skewness, lag-1 autocorrelation), predicted by an 8.5k-parameter
head on the predictor trunk and injected only through the zero-initialized
width gate. Nothing can collapse: the target is a fixed function of the
data. Pre-registered witnesses held: predicted-vs-realized log-RMS
correlation 0.63 (kill threshold 0.3); prediction-to-target std ratio 0.7
against 0.3--0.4 for the signal path, a finding in itself
(\emph{volatility is more predictable than its realization}); the twin
run's invariance loss 4--5\% \emph{better} with z, the feared siphoning
inverted.

The decision rule was registered before results: (a) beat the champion,
z enters the bundle; (b) within 0.5 CRPS points with materially better
coverage, z stays out, becomes the paper ablation; (c) worse, attributed
without appeal. Outcome: (b). Table~\ref{tab:esjepa} carries the section's
main result.

Read the table bottom-up. The gate does not modulate the fan; it
\emph{drives} it: fine-tuning co-adapted the organs, base softplus widths
converging to a near-worst-case envelope and the gate to conditional
sharpening (negative gains on calm windows, the opposite of the widener we
designed). Within this model, four latent dimensions carry the entire
calibration. Two honesty notes: the counterfactual trained without z is the
champion at 0.596, so z is not worth 18.7 leaderboard points, it is worth
nominal coverage at equal CRPS; and ``z on a healthy pretrain'' is open,
not refuted, an ablation the v3 lineage can host.

\subsection{What the judge knows}
\label{sec:t1t2}

On a fully owned testbed (linear thermostat, hidden action, history under a
bang-bang policy: observational confounding in miniature), discrimination
AUCs: time-reversed futures \textbf{0.808}, morphology violations
\textbf{0.935}, state-jump discontinuities \textbf{0.556}, chance. The
judge knows the arrow of time and the shape of trajectories; it accepts a
teleported future. Consistently, $E$ as a planning \emph{regularizer}
fails a fair test: the dormant term changes nothing (violations 78.7\% vs
80.0\%), the active term hurts (78.7\% $\to$ 82.4\%). Three concordant
runs: \emph{the energy is a classifier, not a regularizer}. Planning
prudence must come from predicted uncertainty (fan + z), converging with
\citet{henaff2019mpur}.
