\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

One seed per arm, everywhere, including the objective duel; the registered
three-seed protocol is unfunded. We mitigate with paired designs, bootstrap
intervals and pre-registered directional predictions; we do not solve it.
The body gap is real: 0.54 vs 0.47 on the 81 non-tail configurations, and
the $0.597 \pm 0.002$ triple convergence reads as a capacity bound at
1.14M, an interpretation until a larger model tests it. The harness is a
restatement of the official protocol with measured convention drift (zero
MASE, $\sim$2.7\% CRPS), and all ranks are against one frozen snapshot
(2026-08-22). The pre-v3 corpus observation count was never instrumented
(1.7--4B). The short-series block remains geometry-bound; the registered
fix (crop-and-pad augmentation) is designed, not built. Scope: univariate
by choice; no covariates; judge results are measured on this architecture's
checkpoints only.
