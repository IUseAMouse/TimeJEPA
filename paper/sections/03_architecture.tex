\paragraph{Geometry, set by measurement.}
Context 1024, native horizon 256, patch 16 stride 8. A dedicated round took
the seven-dataset average MASE from 1.365 to 1.159 and located a flat
context optimum (512 to 1280 within 5\%, against 33\% before). The prior
brittleness had a mechanism: skill peaked exactly at the training context
length (electricity: $+28.5\%$ at 384, $-103.8\%$ at 768); the model
memorizes a patch count. The cure is per-batch geometry randomization
(context left-truncated to $\{128..1024\}$, horizon to $\{64..256\}$,
$p{=}0.5$ each); per-batch keeps tensors rectangular, and RoPE makes the
encoder length-agnostic. A scratch control showed the robustness comes from
randomization at fine-tuning time, not from pretraining.

\paragraph{Normalization.}
\label{sec:normalization}
Two composed stages, fitted on the context only:
\begin{equation}
x' = \operatorname{arcsinh}\!\left(\tfrac{x - \mathrm{med}(c)}{s(c)}\right),
\quad
s(c) = \begin{cases}
1.4826\,\mathrm{MAD}(c) & \text{if } > 0.01\,\sigma(c),\\
\max(0.1\,\sigma(c), 10^{-3}) & \text{otherwise,}
\end{cases}
\label{eq:robustscale}
\end{equation}
then RevIN in compressed space. Each term answers a pathology: MAD/arcsinh
for spikes ($\times$100 spikes in VM traces crush $\sigma$-scaled signals;
precedent \citep{cohen2024toto}); the floor for near-constant contexts
(RevIN scale $\sim$0.003 projects targets to thousands of sigmas; arcsinh
makes them ordinary numbers); the conditional fallback because an
unconditional $\max$ would let a spike re-inflate the scale, the exact
pathology MAD exists to ignore. The floor value matters: 29\% of bitbrains
contexts have MAD exactly zero, and an earlier $10^{-8}$ floor overflowed
float32 through $\sinh$ (CRPS $\sim 10^{10}$). Arcsinh is strictly
monotone, so quantiles are equivariant and the median commutes with the
inverse. The inverse additionally clamps to
$[\min(c)-10w,\ \max(c)+10w]$: a half-trained head once emitted
$|z|\approx15$ in compressed space, and $\sinh$ turned it into a CRPS of
$1.8\times10^{7}$ on one configuration, $\times$1.19 on the whole
aggregate; Chronos bounds its vocabulary at $\pm15\sigma$ for the same
reason \citep{ansari2024chronos}.

One theorem, used twice: median and MAD are 1-homogeneous, so the composed
normalization quotients the entire positive affine group. Scale
augmentation is provably inert (pinned by test), and the only surviving
group element is the sign, which is why flip is the one test-time symmetry
worth having (Section~\ref{sec:protocol}).

\paragraph{Encoder and EMA target.}
Three pre-norm blocks \citep{xiong2020preln}, RoPE \citep{su2024rope}, 4
heads, ff 512, non-causal. The target encoder is an EMA copy on the
BYOL/MoCo~v3 cosine schedule, $\tau: 0.996 \to 1$
\citep{grill2020byol,chen2021mocov3}.

\paragraph{Predictor.}
\label{sec:predictor}
Two blocks (lighter than the encoder, the I-JEPA convention). Input: 127
context embeddings concatenated with 31 learned future queries; the last 31
outputs are the predicted latents $\hat z$. The query table is oversized
(50 rows) because slicing past a table's size silently returns fewer rows:
in an early version, missing queries were replaced by context embeddings
and trained as predictions, corrupting every horizon above 136 steps with
valid shapes throughout (Appendix~\ref{app:pitfalls}). Horizon extension at
fine-tuning time grows the table by merging trained rows into a longer one.
An optional zero-initialized FiLM conditions the queries on a scalar (built
for cross-resolution, reused for actions in
Section~\ref{sec:worldmodel}); zero-init makes the unconditioned model
exactly the baseline.

\paragraph{Quantile head.}
\label{sec:quantile-head}
Nine quantiles per step, three commitments. (1) Direct quantiles because
GIFT ranks on CRPS $\approx$ mean pinball over a grid: optimize the metric,
no proxy; and MASE is minimized by the median, which pinball at
$\tau{=}0.5$ estimates exactly. (2) Cross-attention back to the context
embeddings, because the MSE-trained predictor converges to
$\mathbb{E}[z\mid \mathrm{ctx}]$, a conditional mean (measured: predicted
variance 0.6 vs target 0.95); dispersion lives in the context, not in
$\hat z$. (3) Monotone fan by construction: the median is regressed
directly, other levels are median $\pm$ cumulative softplus widths;
crossing is impossible rather than penalized. The ESJEPA gate multiplies
the widths by $e^{g}$ from a zero-initialized linear map of the predicted
residual statistics: exact identity at init, median untouchable, so MASE is
structurally invariant and any CRPS delta is attributable to spread alone.

\paragraph{Rollout beyond 256 steps: propagate the fan, not the median.}
Median feedback has a measured bias: the median path is smoother than any
trajectory, so intervals shrink with horizon (exchange h720 band width
0.267 vs a truth growing like $\sqrt{h}$). We propagate all nine levels,
copy $k$ continuing from its own level $k$ (comonotone coupling), marginal
fan = per-step sort. The assumption, stated honestly: perfect rank
dependence across rolls. Deterministic, no RNG, and it moved the average
fan gain at h720 from $-4\%$ to $-16\%$ WQL with band widths finally
increasing in depth.
