\section{Evaluation protocol and fairness}
\label{sec:protocol}

\paragraph{One checkpoint, one configuration.}
Every GIFT-Eval number comes from a single checkpoint and a single model
configuration serving all 97 configurations: same weights, same context,
same decoding, horizons beyond 256 by the model's own rollout. No
per-regime variants (the TTM family publishes several). Nothing is tuned,
calibrated or selected on GIFT-Eval data; the only instrument that reads
GIFT outputs is a read-only coverage diagnostic.

\paragraph{Harness.}
The official protocol is restated next to its upstream sources, not
imported: prediction lengths per frequency and term, up to 20
non-overlapping windows over the last 10\% of each series, channels split,
the 97 configurations pinned as a literal so upstream drift fails loudly.
MASE uses each series' entire past \citep{hyndman2006mase}; CRPS is
weighted quantile loss over nine levels, pooled per configuration
\citep{gneiting2007scoring,alexandrov2020gluonts}; missing targets masked,
never imputed; aggregates are geometric means of ratios vs seasonal naive.
We print two normalizations side by side, against the official
seasonal-naive numbers and against seasonal naive through our own code
path; the gap measures our convention drift (zero on MASE, $\sim$2.7\% on
CRPS). Context preparation truncates on the left: right-padding would
repeat the last value, fabricating observations exactly where they hurt. A
full 97-configuration pass takes about four minutes on one 3090 (measured
3\,min\,30), which is what makes evaluation-based checkpoint selection
affordable.

\paragraph{Test-time augmentation: the sign flip, and nothing else.}
For a distributional forecast the correct mirror average is
\begin{equation}
\hat{q}_\tau(x) = \tfrac{1}{2}\left[\, q_\tau(x) - q_{1-\tau}(-x) \right],
\label{eq:flip}
\end{equation}
since $q_\tau(-X) = -q_{1-\tau}(X)$: the fan negates and reverses across
levels, ordering preserved. Precedents: TimesFM's
\texttt{force\_flip\_invariance}, YingLong
\citep{das2024timesfm,yinglong2025}. Table~\ref{tab:tta} shows why flip is
alone: the affine group is exhausted by theorem and by measurement.

\paragraph{Fairness rules.}
Every aggregate appears plain and with flip, so the reader prices the
augmentation separately; single-checkpoint TTA is within the leaderboard's
declared norms and we refuse multi-checkpoint ensembling. No number without
its protocol; in-domain lineage labeled as such. Budget: three RTX~3090s,
stated wherever a comparison invokes scale.
